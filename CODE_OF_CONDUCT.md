# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in OpenBlur to be welcoming, professional, and focused on reproducible technical work. We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, or sharing another person’s private media or data.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence, and trade-offs rather than people.
- Use minimal, non-personal media fixtures when reporting issues.
- Respect security-disclosure and licensing obligations.
- Accept constructive review and help keep discussions accessible.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and may result in a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or ban, depending on severity and context.
